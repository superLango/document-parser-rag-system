# 1.0 范围及定义

# SCOPE AND DEFINITION

本质保要求适用于中广核项目中所有质保级的物项（包括核级焊材）和服务的采购 活动，如 C1a 、 C1b 、 C2 。

This Quality Assurance requirement is applicable for CNPEC projects with QA classification （ include nuclear class welding material ） , such as C1a 、 C1b 、 C2.

买方：哈电集团（秦皇岛）重型装备有限公司

Vendee: HQC

CNPEC ：中广核工程有限公司

NNSA ：中华人民共和国国家核安全局

质量证明文件：指采购文件中所要求的 CMTR 、 MTR 、 EOMR 、合格证、符合性声明、 完工文件或数据报告。

Quality certificate document ： The CMTR, MTR, EOMR, Certificate of conformance, Certificate of compliance, Final Record Package, or Data Report that is required by procurement document.

核级焊材：指质保等级为 Q1 、 Q2 、 Q3 ，采购质保等级为 C1a 、 C1b 、 C2 的焊材。

nuclear class welding material: QA classification Q1 、 Q2 、 Q3 , procurement QA classification C1a 、 C1b 、 C2.

# 3.6 对 NNSA 不符合项的管理要求

# 3.6 Management requirements for NNSA non-conformance

当设备属于《民用核安全设备目录》范围内时，供方应同时执行《 84 号文》的规定， 包括设备制造阶段不符合项的分类、报送等要求。

When the equipment falls within the scope of the Catalogue of Civil Nuclear Safety Equipment, the supplier shall simultaneously implement the provisions of Document 84, including the classification and submission of non-conformity items in the manufacturing phase of the equipment.

当《民用核安全设备目录》内的设备或部件报废、焊接返修时，其 NNSA 不符合项 分类至少应定为“一般不符合项”；如属于大量或大面积焊接缺陷和不能在短期内做出 报废决定的情况，应定为较大或重大不符合项。

If the equipment or parts in the Civil Nuclear Safety Equipment Catalog are scrapped or welded and repaired, the classification of NNSA non-conformity items shall be at least "general non-conformity items "; if there are large or large area welding defects and the decision of scrapping can not be made in the short term, they shall be classified as major or terrible non-conformity items.
