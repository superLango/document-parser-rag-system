# 1 Preamble

This Specification is to be read in combination with the General Management Specification [Ref 1] for Service and Supply (GM3S) that constitutes a full part of the technical requirements. In case of conflict, the content of the Specification supersedes the content of GM3S.

# 2 Purpose

This Specification covers supply of ceramic (Alumina and/or Zirconia) components which are mainly used as electrical insulating components in the diagnostic port plugs. The eddy current loops produced during the ITER plasma events, may result into undesirable condition such as local welding, sparking or micro melting at the interface of the sub-assemblies. To mitigate this risk, a need is realized to use electrically insulated ceramic components to hinder the current path. The purpose of this document is to establish the detailed technical requirements for the supply of ceramic components (Alumina and/or Zirconia) for the electrical insulation purpose.

# 6.6 Non Destructive Examination

The surfaces of the ceramic components shall be cleaned so that the required NDE can be performed.

# - Liquid Penetrant Examination (Alcohol test)

All external surfaces of ceramic component shall be examined using solvent penetration test. The solvent used shall be 99% Ethanol 99% +1% MEK (Methyl Ethyl Ketone).

Entire surface of the component/sample shall be covered with Ethanol by a brush. The visual examination shall be performed on the surface to examine for presence of any creak or defect at the surface. For acceptance of the examination, the surfaces shall be free of damage and inclusions.

# - Visual Examination

All external surfaces of ceramic components shall be checked by a visual examination in accordance with written procedure. The procedure shall be agreed with the Customer. The blocks shall be free of scale, strings, tears, nicks or other injurious defects.

Surfaces shall be thoroughly examined during all phases of production to check the ceramic components. All surfaces shall be plane, uniform and free from wrinkles, buckles, blowholes, tears, cracks and inclusions. Allowable on-surfaces defects are: chips with depth not more than 1.5 mm and length not more than 5 mm; scratches with depth not more than 0.3 mm, the length is not regulated.

A visual examination shall be performed on all parts. When the examination indicates the presence of unacceptable defects on the block, it will be rejected.

# 6.8 Use of Glaze coating or Sealant

# 6.8.1 For in-vessel components

Use of glaze coating or sealant (to improve the electrical insulation properties) for any of the in- vessel component is strictly prohibited. This is because the glaze or sealant will not be able to withstand the ultra-high vacuum condition and may tear open during operation. In addition, the sealant material is not approved by IO for use in in-vessel ultra-high vacuum condition.

<!-- page: 13 -->

# 6.8.2 For ex-vessel components

Ex-vessel components are exposed to the atmospheric and presence of air in the prose of ceramic component form a favourable condition for ionization of the air. The use of certain sealant will fill the prose of ceramic components and therefore may provide better insulation. Use of glaze coating could also be useful in improving the electrical insulation.

Therefore, in case supplier realise the need to add glaze or sealant to improve the electrical insulation of the ex-vessel ceramic components exposed to atmosphere, supplier shall evaluate and propose the suitable sealant to IO with enough detail about sealant seeking IOs approval.
