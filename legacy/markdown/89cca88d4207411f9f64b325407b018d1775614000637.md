# SUBJECT

This document, produced as a complement to document DMF 90000-01, defines the inspection requirements applicable to forged or cold-formed Class A parts (drawn, hydroformed, etc.) followed by the final heat treatment of the material

NOTE: If no criterion class is specified on the drawing, class A is applicable.

# SCOPE

This document is applicable whenever it is referenced on a part drawing or order, unless otherwise specified in the said documents.

# DEFINITIONS

DEFINITIONS Formed part : Part formed cold using the selected method of deformation (drawing, hydroforming, etc.), and treated with the final heat treatment of the material.

# MATERIALS

The following materials are concerned:

 Martensitic steels (documents DMD 00XX);

 Low-alloy steels (documents DMD 01XX) ;

 High-alloy martensitic and austenitic steels (documents DMD 02XX and documents DMD 03XX) [See list in appendix 7];

 Heat resistant alloys (documents DMD 04XX) ;

 Light alloys (documents DMD 05XX) ;

 Titanium alloys (documents DMD 07XX).

# DELIVERY CONDITION OF PARTS

Unless otherwise specified on the drawing or order, the parts are delivered:

 In the standard heat treatment condition, corresponding to the procurement state of the DMD concerned. Refer to the DMD to find out the exact heat treatment conditions (temperature, time, etc.)

 With the following surface condition:

# INSPECTIONS TO PERFORM

The choice between using a part or an integral test piece for the master part or periodic cut-up test is left to the production source, subject to the agreement of Snecma Quality, but the position of the integral test piece must be frozen by the production source with the agreement of Snecma Quality when the master part is made.

The test areas for Master Parts and Periodic Cut-ups are to be determined in the framework of industrial substantiation of the part.

For experimental parts, the master part and the periodic cut-ups are not required.

For experimental parts, the master part and the periodic cut-ups are not required. The following inspections are presented by family of material in the form of tables. (Appendices 1 to 5). They are to be performed when the inspection criteria are mentioned in the referenced DMD.

Intermediate inspections may be performed by the production source to avoid the industrial risk of launching the production of non-compliant parts.

# APPENDIX 9 INFORMATIVE

# FREQUENCY OF PERIODIC CUT-UP TESTS

The aim of these tests is to guarantee compliance with the DMF requirements by verifying the stability of the process. To this end, the production source must put in place a system of statistical tracking, which can also serve to substantiate a reduction in the inspection frequencies under the conditions of DMF90000-01.

The frequency is indicated for reference purposes for the periodic cut-ups:

- 25 th , 50 th ,100 th part or their integral test rings - Beyond the 100 th part, the frequency is left to the initiative of Snecma Quality in

agreement with the production source.

It may be modified in accordance with DMF90000-01.
