# 1 Purpose

This technical appendix covers the general requirements for material and includes the procurement specifications of the main base materials to be used in the manufacturing of Common Components (CC).

# 2 Scope

Technical appendix covering the general requirements for the procurement of materials activities to be performed during the manufacturing of Common Components (base and filler materials). It also includes the Product Procurement Specifications of the main base materials.

Technical appendix covering the general requirements for the procurement of materials activities to be performed during the manufacturing of Common Components (base and filler materials). It also includes the Product Procurement Specifications of the main base materials. The first part is devoted to establishing the general requirements applicable to material procurement activities under RCC-MR 2007 Code. The two main ways described in RCC-MR 2007 that are applicable in the scope of this Technical Specification for material procurement are outlined as well as the product qualification route included in RCC-MR 2007 Code. Material traceability and approval requirements are also described. In the main part of this appendix, Product Procurement Specifications (PPSs) including all requirements that are applicable to different material products that take part on the manufacturing of In-vessel components are introduced. Additional requirements regarding the necessity of product qualification are also stated. Finally, requirements related to material identification, traceability and marking are described.

In the main part of this appendix, Product Procurement Specifications (PPSs) including all requirements that are applicable to different material products that take part on the manufacturing of In-vessel components are introduced. # 5 Generalities of material procurement

# 5.1 Procurement of materials under RCC-MR 2007

Material procurement activities shall be performed according to RM 0100 in which among others, there are proposed two main ways (pertaining this Technical Specification) to procure products and parts within the scope of Subsection C of RCC-MR 2007.

The first one is based on what Code identifies as Reference Procurement Specifications (RPS), defined in RM 0112. They can be divided into product specifications and part specifications. The definition of each is essentially that part specifications are focused to and specific item of an equipment (i.e. forgings for tube plates in steam generators) and product specifications are oriented to all families of products covered in the scope of the specification (i.e. Product procurement specification: class 1, 2 and 3 X2CrNiMo17-12-2 controlled nitrogen content austenitic stainless steel plates). There are a limited number of part specifications and they are also used to specify the way in which these parts should be manufactured.

# 9 Positive Material Identification (PMI)

Positive Material PMI testing constitutes a chemical composition evaluation of the material, meeting the requirements of the Product Procurement Specification, to confirm that the material which will be placed into service is consistent with the selected or specified grade.

[B1_03-RQ-043]

PMI testing if needed, shall be performed at a point in time that ensures proper material is used in the fabrication of an identifiable assembly.

Usually, this is during delivery material acceptance at Supplier's factory, fabrication or immediately prior to fabrication.

Testing performed by material Supplier of raw material or loose components is not considered to be PMI testing.

PMI testing can be witnessed by an inspector with a notification by the Supplier.

[B1_03-RQ-044]

PMI testing shall not substitute required material test reports and certificates.

[B1_03-RQ-045]

PMI shall be performed by qualified personnel with operator's records being available to the Client for review and approval.

The methods to be used (X-ray fluorescence analysis or optical emission spectrograph for lighter elements) are suitable to identify the material by quantitative measurement of the major alloying elements in the product procurement specification. As an alternative, a chemical analysis of samples cut out of pieces (in the pre-machined condition) requiring PMI may be used to check the alloying elements of the material.

Applicable Standards covering methods for PMI are:

 EN 10315:2006 Routine method for analysis of high alloy steel by X-ray Fluorescence Spectrometry (XRF) by using a nearby technique.  ASTM E1476-04(2010) Standard Guide for Metals Identification, Grade Verification, and Sorting.
