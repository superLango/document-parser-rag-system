# 1 Scope

This Specification covers grade X6NiCrTiMoVB25-15-2 (No. 1.4980) structural hardening austenitic stainless steel hot rolled or forged bars with diameter not greater than 160 mm for the ITER Common components manufacturing (CCM) Contract. The products are intended for bolting.

This Specification is based on the product procurement specification EN 10269 which is supplemented by RCC-MR 2007, RM 3551 on Class 1, 2 and 3 bars for bolting, plus additional requirements arising from the features of the ITER Common Components (CC).

As Common Components (CC) are classified as RCC-MR Class 2 structures, rolled or forged bars and semi-finished products are also considered for equipment Class 2 (in accordance with RCC-MR classification).

The amount of the steel bars to be procured shall be specified by the supplier and shall include appropriate contingency to face unexpected difficulties, to remake rejected parts and to repair parts with insufficient quality.

The supply covers the following items:

 Manufacture of the total quantity of stainless steel bar grade X6NiCrTiMoVB25-15-2 (No. 1.4980).

 Organization of quality at works.

 Elaboration of all procedures required for the manufacturing, inspection (including analyses), packaging, storage and delivery.

 Time schedules and documentation.

 To perform all the inspections and tests during and after manufacturing envisaged in this specification.

 Storage, packaging and delivery.

# 6.3 Magnetic permeability

The relative magnetic permeability of the finished forged bars shall be measured at room temperature after heat treatment. The value measured shall be equal or lower than 1.03 for fields of over 80000A/m (1000Oe) as per test method 2 or measured with a low permeability indicator as per method 3 of ASTM A342-14. One specimen per heat treatment shall be taken from position close to mechanical test specimens. # 6.4 Structure

A micrographic examination, with photographs, shall be performed parallel to the maximum direction of extension. The structure must be homogeneous.

# 6.4.1 Grain size

The grain size number determined in accordance with RMC 1000 of RCC-MR 2007 (EN ISO 643) shall not be less than 3. The presence of a few grains of index 1 or 2 is tolerated. Test shall be performed per lot (test unit as for definition in EN 10269). The determination is performed on test samples taken from the immediate vicinity of the mechanical test samples.

Test shall be performed per lot (test unit as for definition in EN 10269).

Bars shall be delivered in the heat treated condition compatible with further bolting manufacturing.

# 7.2.1 Heat treatment for mechanical properties

The heat treatments for mechanical properties are given in Table 4 and Table B1 of the standard EN 10269 for grade X6NiCrTiMoVB25-15-2. Solution heat treatment shall consist of holding at a temperature of 970°C to 990°C for at least 1 hour. This is followed by oil quenching then precipitation treatment at a range of 710°C to730°C; the minimum time for precipitation treatment shall be for 16 hours. Then air cooling. The thermal cycles shall be recorded and the records kept shall be presented in the test report.

# 7.2.2 Machining - surface conditions

The parts shall be machined to its as-delivered profile. The surface condition determined in accordance with RMC 7200 shall meet the following requirement: roughness Ra shall not exceed 12.5 microns.
