# 1 Scope

This Specification covers 316L(N)-IG austenitic stainless steel forgings and drop forgings with unit weight of not more than 10 tonnes as-delivered condition for the ITER Common components manufacturing(CCM)Contract.

Generally 316L(N)-IG steel is grade 316L steel with narrower alloying element ranges and controlled impurities. The closest analogy is X2CrNiMo17-12-2 controlled nitrogen content austenitic stainless steel described in the RCC-MR Code, Edition 2007.
