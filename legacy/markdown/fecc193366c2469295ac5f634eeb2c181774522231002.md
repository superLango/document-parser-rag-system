# 3. Purpose

The aim of this guideline is to define general packing, labeling and dispatch requirements towards Contractor of Siemens Energy AB.

The instructions described below are minimum requirements and product specific requirements could be added in agreement with SEAB. This instruction is the basis for the acceptance of the packing by Siemens or its representative. Following the instructions does not discharge the contractor from liability for damages incurred as a result of poor packing or inadequate corrosion protection. In the case of a damage incurred due to packing defects, this document will be considered state of the art in disputes.

4. Scope of applicability This document shall apply for all goods that shall be delivered to SEAB in Finspong or Trollhättan (for goods delivered to harbour or site, E1B100052679 applies). The contractor shall impose the obligations of this document on its Subcontractors (if any).

# 5. Procedure

When designing the transport packing, it is essential to consider that:

- The packages shall allow safe and appropriate handling, lifting (e.g. sufficient lifting lugs, slinging points) and load securing.

- Goods heavier than 25 kilos must always be delivered on pallet. If other is agreed with Siemens, the package should have a bottom clearance of at least 100 mm for entering the arms of a forklift.

# 5.2.10 Sensitive goods

For goods or articles which are manufactured with fine tolerances extra precautions must be made to secure protection during transport. E.g. threaded bolts should be covered with a protective sleeve and holes must be protected to avoid dirt and mechanical damage. When these articles are consolidated they must be fixed, secured and separated to avoid mechanical damage due to scratching, scuffing and indentations between articles or other damage created by forces that could arise during transport, transhipment or handling.

# 5.2.11 Use of ISO-containers

ISO-Containers could only be used if agreed with SEAB. SEAB are not able to handle or receive containers in Finspong or Trollhättan and for that reason a third-party logistics provider must assist in unloading ISO-Containers and transship the goods onto pallets. The handling procedure must be set before delivery with ISO-Containers takes place. Delivery by ISO-Container must be approved by SEAB prior to delivery, please contact your purchaser at SEAB for approval and further questions. The ISO-Containers shall be in appliance with the applicable national and international regulations, meet the requirements of the loaded goods as well as the requirements for securing the load and shall be both resistant to wind and waterproof.

# 5.3 Conservation

5.3 Conservation Corrosion protection is to be defined by the contractor in dependence on the known sensitivity of his products if not given on the drawing or by SEAB. Respective passages, expected duration of transport from contractor to SEAB and if any specific storage requirements are set for the specific delivery shall be considered.

Temporary corrosion protection methods such as: protective coating, vapour corrosion inhibitor (VCI) and airtight packing shall follow pertinent international standards and may not impair the functional capacity and application purpose of the products. It must be possible to completely and economically remove and dispose of any protective systems that are used and the chemical substances should not have a toxic effect, cause allergic reactions or be detrimental for the environment in any way.
