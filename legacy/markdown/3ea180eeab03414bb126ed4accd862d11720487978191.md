6.2 例如：测定冲击值为20.6J的脆性转变温度。

6.2.1 如果冲击值为20.6J的脆性转变温度在+20℃～-20℃之间，20.6J的脆性转变温度应在图上标出。（例1，图1中材料A的脆性转变温度为-17℃。）

6.2.1 If the impact resistance of 20.6J appears in the chosen temperature range of -20 $ ^{\circ} $ C to +20 $ ^{\circ} $ C, the transition temperature at 20.6J has to be mentioned as the result, (Example 1, see diagram below: Material A with a transition temperature of -17 $ ^{\circ} $ C).

6.2.2 如果-20℃的冲击值大于20.6J，则说明其脆性转变温度低于-20℃。（例2，下图中材料B的脆性转变温度低于-20℃。）

6.2.2 If the impact resistance at -20 $ ^{\circ} \mathrm{C} $ is above 20.6J it should be stated that the transition temperature is below -20 $ ^{\circ} \mathrm{C} $ .(Example 2, see diagram below: Material B with transition temperature < -20 $ ^{\circ} \mathrm{C} $ ).

# 7 要求

7.1 在 I&T 计划中应规定进行脆性转变温度试验。

# 7Requirements

7.1 The required impact resistance, the transition temperature had to be determined for, has to be defined in the I & T Plan.

7.2 除本标准的要求外，HEC交货条件中要求的冲击试验值也应满足要求。

7.2 Besides this specification all other requirements according HEC delivery instructions regarding Charpy impact testing have to be also fulfilled.

# 8 报告

# 8 Report

试验报告应包括以下内容：

The report should contain:

一 根据ASTM 370中27章要求的所有数据；

— All data requireed according ASTM A370 chapter 27.

一 本标准第 5 章描述的图表；

— Diagram as described in chapter 5 of this specification.

一 脆性转变温度；

— Transition temperature.

一 HEC交货条件中要求的相关结果。

— Results regarding requirements according HEC delevery instruction.
