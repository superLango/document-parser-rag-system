# 1.0 SCOPE:

1.1 This specification covers the heat treatment (HT-45) required for Inconel 718 alloy to develop a minimum tensile strength of 185,000 psi (1275 MPa). The material is normally purchased in the solution annealed condition (HT-45A) having a maximum hardness of 277 Brinell (28 RC). After heat treatment (HT-45) it will develop a minimum hardness of 331 Brinell (36 RC).

2.0 HEAT TREATMENT:

# 2.0 HEAT TREATMENT:

2.1 Solution Heat Treatment HT-45A:

2.1.1 Where material is purchased in a condition other than the solution treated condition or where an intermediate anneal is required to eliminate the effect of cold work or welding, the following heat treatment is to be used:

2.1.1.1 Use a vacuum, dry nitrogen or reducing (2% minimum CO) atmosphere, if the parts are finished.

2.1.1.2 Heat to 1750 - 1850 °F (955 - 1010 °C).

2.1.1.3 Hold at the selected temperature $ \pm 2 5 \, \mathrm{^o F} $ $ (1 4 \, \mathrm{^o C}) $ for one (1) hour per inch (25 mm) of thickest cross section.

2.1.1.4 Air cool or oil quench. Do not slow cool.

2.1.2 Vendors are to use the paragraph 2.1 solution heat treatment when supplying Siemens with G-44, C-56 or C-56A materials.

2.2 Precipitation Hardening HT-45: Precipitation hardening of solution treated material:

2.2.1 Heat to 1325 $ \pm 1 5 $ $ ^{\circ} \mathrm{F} $ $ ( 7 1 8 \pm 8 $ $ ^{\circ} \mathrm{C} $ ) and hold at temperature for eight (8) hours.

2.2.2 Furnace cool to $ 1 1 5 0 \pm1 5 \degree F $ （ $ 6 2 1 \pm8 \degree C $ ）.

2.2.3 Hold at 1150 $ ^{\circ} $ F (621 $ ^{\circ} $ C) for eight (8) hours.

2.2.4 Air or furnace cool under atmosphere as required obtaining the desired surface finish.

NOTE: The accumulated time of the 1325 °F hold, the 1150 °F hold, and the furnace cool between temperatures must not exceed 18 hours in order to prevent overaging.

2.2.5 Where the reducing atmosphere is used, a thin green, black oxide film will be left on the surface. In the case where this heat treatment is used as an intermediate anneal for rolled thread studs, this oxide layer should be removed by sand blasting prior to the final rolling pass.

2.2.6 If the atmosphere is oxidizing, a heavy black scale will result.

# 3.0 MECHANICAL PROPERTIES:

3.1 Hardness after precipitation hardening (HT-45): 36 HRC, minimum.

4.0 QUALITY AND INSPECTION:

4.1 Verify hardness on a minimum of one piece per heat treat lot.

# 5.0 CERTIFICATION:

5.1 The supplier is responsible for material certification and/or certificate of conformance to the requirements of this specification, as directed on the component definition (drawing) and supplier quality requirements.
