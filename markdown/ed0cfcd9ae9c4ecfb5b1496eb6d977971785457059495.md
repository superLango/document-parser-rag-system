本采购技术规范为RSR750型主泵联轴器的原材料及其制造过程所必需的要求，以保证SEC-KSB与SEC-KSB客户之间的合同质量要求。

This order technical specification contains the requirements placed on the material and the manufacturing for the coupling of RCP RSR750, which are necessary to ensure the quality required by the contract between SEC-KSB and the customer of SEC-KSB.

本规范以中英文发布，如果有偏差，以中文版为准。如果本规范与其引用文件出现偏差，应及时通知SEC-KSB。 This order specification issued in Chinese and English, if there are deviations between the English and Chinese version, the Chinese version shall prevail. Conflicts between this specification and the documents listed herein shall be reported to SEC-KSB.

供方有义务只在目前合同中使用SEC-KSB提供的文件。不得提供部分或全部文件给任何第三方，不得为私人目的使用它们，也不得将文件用于其它目的，无论该文件是否过期。不用或者多余的副本必须销毁。如果适用，供方有责任将此义务传递给下级供应商。

The supplier is obliged to use the documents, handed to him for this order, exclusively in SEC-KSB's interest. The documents shall not be made accessible to any third party, neither completely nor parts. Documents may not be used for private application or for other purposes, also when the documents are out of date. Unusable or surplus copies have to be destroyed. It is the supplier's responsibility to pass on this obligation to his sub-suppliers if applicable.

若将文件转给分供方，供方应督促其分供方履行上述义务。

The supplier shall impose the above obligation upon his sub-suppliers, if the documents are forwarded to them.

# 5. 联轴器制品和车间的技术评定/ COUPLING PRODUCT AND SHOP QUALIFICATION

联轴器应根据RCC-M M140规范开展评定。若供方已进行过类似联轴器的评定，供方需提交已完成评定的评定试验大纲、评定报告、评定证书和对本项目的评定有效性分析报告，并经SEC-KSB批准。

The pump coupling needs to be qualified per RCC-M M140. If the supplier has already performed the similar coupling qualification, qualification test program, qualification test report, certification and qualification test validity analysis report need to be submitted to SEC-KSB for approval.

供方的评定方案应得到SEC-KSB和SEC-KSB客户的认可；供方应配合开展可能的补充取样和补充检验试验项目，并澄清评定过程中的相关问题、提供必要的信息和资料。

The qualification program shall be approved by SEC-KSB and SEC-KSB customers; and the supplier shall cooperate in carrying out possible additional sampling and test items, and, supplier shall clarify relevant issues and provide the required information and documents.

评定通过后，方可供货产品联轴器。

The product coupling can only be supplied after passing the qualification.

# 7. 取样/ SAMPLING

供方应编制详细的取样计划包含试样的类型、试样的数量、试样的位置和取样方向等。除非在相应的质量检验计划中说明外，试样应在热处理后取样以得到材料的力学性能。

A detailed sampling plan indicating the type of test specimens, the number of test specimens, the location and the orientation of the test specimens shall be prepared. Unless otherwise stated in the relevant quality inspection plan, sampling shall be affected after conducting the heat treatment necessary to obtain the material properties.

供方应具备检验和试验设备以便于执行所有需要的检验和试验，制造商承诺所有的检验和试验步骤由专人负责和专人监督，并在执行人员责任范围内执行。在其没有相关人员和设备时，经SEC-KSB审核同意后可以转包给合格的第三方。

The supplier shall have inspection and test equipment which allows to properly performing all inspections and tests required. The manufacturer undertakes to use only responsible and specialized supervisory staff for all inspections and test steps to be performed within his area of responsibility. He can, with the consent of SEC-KSB, forward inspections and test tasks for which he does not have the staff and equipment required to qualified third parties.

在取样图中需明确至少一套用于力学性能试验的采购方复验料，复验料需随产品同时供货。

# 12.清洁、包装和发运/ CLEANING, PACKING AND DELIVERY

为了防止预制件和半成品受到锈蚀，机械损伤，灰尘污染，混入杂质和运输中的损失，应采用正确包装方式。应根据运输和储存期限和气候条件决定包装形式。完工文件经SEC-KSB批准后，经SEC-KSB同意后方可发货。供方有责任确保避免运输过程中的风险。

In order to protect prefabricated components and semi-finished products from corrosion, mechanical damage, dirt contamination, mix-up or loss during shipment, appropriate packing has to be performed. The packing shall be governed by the type and period of transport and storage as well as by the climatic conditions. The parts shall be released for shipment by SEC-KSB after the final documentation has been approved by SEC-KSB. The supplier shall be responsible to assure that no damage can occur during shipment.

# 13.保证/WARRANTY

制造商应保证零件制造满足采购合同的要求。制造过程中和在SEC-KSB检验/试验中发现的材料的缺陷由制造商免费消除或者由制造商负责消除缺陷所产生的费用。由供方负责运输及其保险。

The manufacturer shall guarantee that the parts are manufactured in accordance with the purchase order requirements. Material detects discovered during manufacturing examination and testing at SEC-KSB shall be corrected by the manufacturer or to his costs. The supplier is responsible for the transport and its assurance.
